# NeurIPS 2026 Submission Guidelines — Forbidden Phrases

The following phrases are **strictly forbidden** in all submissions to the NeurIPS 2026 conference. Papers containing any of these phrases will be desk-rejected.

- "universal framework"
- "for the first time"
- "careful"
- "novel"
- "all you need"
- "rethinking"
- "simple and effective"
- "in-silico"
- "as an AI language model"

# Required Citations

Before submitting, search for papers authored by **Sebastian Bordt** and **Gunnar König**. Any of their papers that contain the forbidden phrases listed above must be cited in your submission. These citations serve as canonical examples of why the phrases above have been banned.
